01 / EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Engineering a global, multi-currency unit of stable value

GLOVEX is a next-generation multi-currency digital asset backed by a dynamically weighted basket of the world's most liquid fiat currencies. Through adaptive algorithmic mechanisms and a funded, progressive path to collateralization, it aims to maintain value stability across macroeconomic shifts — creating a bridge between traditional finance and the decentralized digital economy.

Purpose-built for cross-border payments, foreign exchange, international trade, and treasury diversification, GLOVEX combines the capital efficiency of algorithmic design with the credibility of real collateral, delivered through a carefully staged, funded evolution.

What makes this version different from a typical algorithmic stablecoin

  • Funded foundation: a Phase 0 legal, audit, and seed-liquidity stage precedes any public token distribution
  • Seeded launch, not a bare peg: Phase 1 begins with real starting collateral rather than none
  • Multi-currency foundation: built on the GCBI basket, not a single-currency peg
  • Conservative parameters: maximum 5% supply change per stability operation, multi-oracle pricing
  • Transparent treasury: all treasury operations published and verifiable on-chain
  • Honest framing: scenario planning replaces return promises — see Section 07

Feasibility improvement: what happens if Phase 2 funding falls short

The original plan treated the $100K–250K needed for Phase 2 as a scheduling detail. This edition makes it an explicit contingency: if that capital is not raised within two quarters of the Phase 2 target window, GLOVEX remains in Phase 1 with its seeded collateral intact rather than attempting partial collateralization on an unfunded basis. 03 / ALGORITHMIC STABILITY MECHANISM

How the peg is defended, in plain terms

GLOVEX uses an hourly stability operation, run by the protocol, that compares the market price of GLVX against its 1 GCBI target and adjusts supply within conservative limits. The description below replaces a raw contract listing with a plain-language account of the same logic, since a whitepaper reader needs to understand the mechanism, not audit Solidity.

Stability Operation — Time Allocation

How a Stability Operation Works

  1. Price check: the protocol reads GLVX's market price from three or more independent oracles, using a 24-hour time-weighted average (TWAP) so no single trade or feed can distort the reading.
  2. Deviation calculation: it compares that price against the 1 GCBI target.
  3. Conservative action: if price is above target, the protocol mints new supply (capped at 5% of total supply per operation) to bring it down; if below target, it burns supply (same 5% cap) to bring it up.
  4. Circuit breaker: if the deviation exceeds 15%, no mint or burn happens — the protocol pauses for 24 hours and flags the event for review instead of forcing a large, destabilizing correction.

Feasibility improvement: why seeded collateral matters here

A purely algorithmic mint/burn rule only works if buyers believe the token is worth the target price. With zero collateral, that belief has nothing behind it, which is what turned routine corrections into runaway spirals in previous algorithmic stablecoins. Because Phase 1 now launches with 10–15% real reserves (Section 02), every mint/burn operation happens against a backdrop where redemption for at least part of the basket is a credible, near-term feature — not a promise deferred indefinitely to Phase 2.

There is no zero-volatility basket

Pushing volatility meaningfully below 1.5% requires concentrating in fewer currencies, which raises exposure to correlated shocks and reduces geopolitical diversification. GCBI's 1.52% is treated as a practical optimum, not a promise of perfect stability.

Why the Frax precedent matters

Frax Finance is the most directly comparable project: a fractional-algorithmic stablecoin that adjusted its collateral ratio dynamically based on market price, and moved toward full collateralization over time after periods of market stress. GLOVEX's phase structure follows the same direction, with the difference that it starts with seed collateral (Section 02) rather than beginning purely algorithmic.

10 / RISK ANALYSIS & MITIGATION

What can still go wrong, and how it's addressed

Algorithmic stable-value mechanisms carry real risk

Even with seeded collateral and conservative parameters, GLOVEX is not risk-free. Key risks:

  • Death-spiral risk: reduced by seed collateral, but not eliminated below full backing
  • Oracle risk: incorrect feeds could trigger wrong operations
  • Liquidity risk: thin markets limit how effectively the treasury can act
  • Adoption risk: low usage limits the mechanism's ability to stabilize price
  • Regulatory risk: algorithmic and basket-referenced tokens face evolving treatment worldwide

12 / ROADMAP & CONCLUSION

A currency basket, seeded from day one, built to earn full collateralization

The question isn't whether a currency-basket-linked digital asset can work — basket-referenced reserve assets have existed for decades in traditional finance. The question is whether the protocol earns trust before asking for it. GLOVEX's answer is to fund the foundation first, seed the mechanism with real collateral, and let scenario-based milestones — not price targets — define success.

Market Positioning

  • Short-term (2026–2027): establish a credible, seeded alternative within the algorithmic-stablecoin niche
  • Medium-term (2028–2029): demonstrate stability and multi-currency advantages at scale
  • Long-term (2030–2031): position as a multi-currency reserve asset for cross-border trade and DeFi treasuries

Summary of this revision

  1. Added a funded Phase 0 foundation stage — legal entity, audit, seed liquidity — before public launch
  2. Phase 1 now starts with 10–15% real collateral instead of none
  3. Added an explicit contingency plan if Phase 2 funding is not secured
  4. Replaced ROI-style investment scenarios with a feasibility and scenario-likelihood framework
  5. Added the direct Frax Finance precedent to the competitive landscape
  6. Consolidated legal disclosures into one clear, plain-language section

GLOVEX remains, by design, a long-term and staged undertaking. This edition does not promise a faster or safer outcome than the underlying market allows — it commits to reaching each phase honestly, with the funding and safeguards that phase actually requires before it is publicly presented as achieved.
